# LSS 0.19.0 release preparation

Prepared 2026-10-14 and restaged the same day after the manual Astra pass. **All six pinned
candidates pass the exact-version local gate.** Identities, counts and the 48 jar hashes are in
the [manifest](../implementation/2026-10-14-v0.19.0-release-preparation.json). The release
branches are pushed to PRs #349–#354. **The release is held for the maintainer's go.** Nothing is
merged, tagged or published.

## What 0.19.0 contains

The [plan](2026-10-13-map-policy-and-xaero-cave-layers-plan.md) records the design, the
review folds and the user decisions.

**Xaero's World Map cave layers (#313):**
- Every column writes the surface. Within min(LOD distance, 128) chunks it also writes the layer
  Xaero shows: a 16-block band, or the Full layer.
- In ceiling dimensions, a bounded client copy of the columns refills the shown layer
  when Xaero switches to it (fill-on-switch, §B10).
  - The copy's size is `integrations.map_ingestion.nether_store_mib` in the experimental sidecar:
    256 MiB by default, and 0 turns it off.
  - `/lss clearcache` deletes it.
- B-II (writing every Nether band all the time) failed its spike and was replaced; §B10.1
  has the numbers.

**Server map-ingestion request (#316):**
- The option is `privacy.request_no_map_ingestion`. It defaults to off and applies on
  reload.
- Clients declare capability bit 8. The server appends `sessionFlags` to a version-20
  SessionConfig only for sessions that declared it, so 0.10–0.18 clients never see a
  trailing byte.
- While the policy is on:
  - clients without the bit get `enabled=false` and one chat line per connection;
  - legacy dialects are refused at the handshake but never revoked mid-session;
  - map-only clients park their session.

**Added in the restage, after the Astra pass:**
- **The 1.21.1 corner seam.** On 1.21.1 only, vanilla's send test
  (`ChunkTrackingView.isWithinDistance`, includeOuter) pads only the dominant axis.
  - So in-view circle-edge cells, (±3,±4)/(±4,±3) at view distance 4, arrive without their
    outward diagonal neighbour.
  - Xaero maps a chunk only when all 8 neighbours are loaded, and LSS excluded those cells as
    vanilla-rendered, so they got neither map data nor LOD.
  - The fix: `SpiralScanner.isVanillaRendered` and `RegionScanner`'s r0 now ask the line's own
    send test whether a cell's whole 8-neighbourhood is delivered.
  - This is a no-op on 1.21.2+, pinned by `VanillaViewExclusionTest`. Axis-edge cells stay
    excluded, so the old re-request loop stays closed.
- **JourneyMap throughput.** Batches now fill JourneyMap's 2 × renderDelay box across up to 8
  regions; the pick is sized from the measured per-column cost.
  - `JMChunkSnapshot.decode` runs ahead on two LSS daemon workers, at most 32 decodes ahead,
    with unstarted work cancelled at batch end.
  - The decode is pure in all 12 bound JourneyMap jars; `docs/implementation/journeymap-bridge.md`
    has the bytecode walk.
  - The wrap, every DataCache call and all rendering stay on JourneyMap's task thread.
  - Dev kill switch: `-Dlss.journeymap.decodeAhead=false`.
- **The B1 "floating strip" was not a reset bug.** The logs show `/lss clearcache`, which keeps
  Voxy's terrain. The strip was the run's first-join Voxy terrain 4.5 km away. No change.

## Validation beyond the gate

- A new client stops writing to the map on a live arm, and resumes on disarm.
- The deny survives a Nether trip (the dimension-change push).
- A 0.18.0 client gets `enabled=false`, one chat line and no LODs. Nothing repeats after 2
  minutes. It is re-offered on disarm and revoked on re-arm.
- A v19-dialect client is refused at the handshake and keeps serving across a live arm.
- A map-only client parks, re-arms and parks again.
- With the policy on and JourneyMap on the client, LOD delivery passes and JourneyMap renders 0
  LSS columns.

**xaero-nether-bands spike** (§B0/§B10.1), final code, on both worlds:
- Each band switch fills 100% of the stored square: 12–14 s on the pre-generated world,
  4–8 s on the trickle world.
- Heap stays at or below 1.19× the B-I run. Reliable load latency is unchanged, and Xaero threw
  no exceptions.
- The scenario's generic soak laws fail because of the scenario's shape:
  - a counter reset on the dimension-change push;
  - law coverage;
  - disk timeouts on the generation-bound world.

  Its verdict is the scenario law, which passed.

## Known limits

- **Overworld and End cave maps:** terrain that arrived while you were outside is not copied into
  cave maps later. `/lss clearcache` while underground backfills it.
- **Seam:** a slight shading seam where Xaero's own nearby cave tiles meet LSS's band-top
  tiles.
- **LAN host:** no cave-layer writes; Xaero builds those layers from the world save.
- **Downgrade:** a server file that sets `privacy.request_no_map_ingestion` won't load on
  0.18 or older.
- **1.21.1 servers behind ViaVersion:** a newer client cannot see the server's send formula, so
  the corner seam remains for that combination.
- **JourneyMap off-heap images:** a batch can touch up to 8 regions × 3 map types (~31 MiB). The
  total is still bounded by JourneyMap's 30 s expiry.
- **Everything listed under the 0.18.0 known limits still applies**, including Voxy having
  no 26.3 release build.
